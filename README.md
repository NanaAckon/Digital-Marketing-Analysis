# Digital-Marketing-Analysis

##  Project Overview

ShopEasy is an online retail platform experiencing declining customer engagement and conversion rates despite increased marketing spend. This project focuses on analyzing marketing performance data and customer feedback to uncover the root causes of poor campaign performance and provide data-driven recommendations to improve engagement, conversions, and customer satisfaction.

The analysis combines campaign performance metrics with unstructured customer feedback data (reviews and social media comments) to deliver actionable business insights.

##  Business Problem

ShopEasy has observed:
- Declining conversion rates despite increased website traffic
- Reduced customer engagement across marketing channels
- Rising marketing costs with low return on investment (ROI)
- Negative customer sentiment reflected in reviews and social media feedback

Management needs a comprehensive analysis to understand what is driving these trends and how to optimize marketing strategies for better performance.

##  Project Objectives And Goals

The primary goals of this project are to:

1. **Increase Conversion Rates**
   - Identify key drop-off points in the customer journey
   - Recommend optimizations to improve purchase completion

2. **Enhance Customer Engagement**
   - Analyze which marketing content types generate the highest engagement
   - Identify underperforming channels and campaigns

3. **Improve Customer Feedback Scores**
   - Extract common themes from customer reviews and social media comments
   - Highlight recurring customer pain points and positive experiences

##  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Conversion Rate**  
  Percentage of website visitors who complete a purchase.

- **Customer Engagement Rate**  
  Level of interaction with marketing content (clicks, likes, shares, comments).

- **Average Order Value (AOV)**  
  Average amount spent per customer transaction.

- **Customer Feedback Score**  
  Average rating derived from customer reviews and sentiment analysis.


  ##  Data Sources

The analysis uses data from multiple sources:

- **Marketing Campaign Data**
  - Customer Data
  - Customer Journey Data
  - Products Data
  - Customer Reviews Data
  - Engagenment Data

##  Tools & Technologies

- **SQL** – Data cleaning And Insights    
- **Python** **(NLTK / TextBlob / VADER)** – Sentiment analysis  
- **Power BI** – Data Visualization (Building an interactive Dashboard) 
- *Microsoft PPT**  Presenting To Stakeholders 

##  Methodology

1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation**
   - Removed duplicates and handled missing values
   - Standardized date formats and categorical variables

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
   - Analyzed trends in conversion rates and engagement over time
   - Compared performance across campaigns and channels

3. **Conversion Funnel Analysis**
   - Identified stages with the highest drop-off rates
   - Assessed potential friction points in the customer journey

4. **Customer Feedback & Sentiment Analysis**
   - Performed sentiment analysis on reviews and comments
   - Extracted common keywords and recurring themes

5. **Insight Generation & Recommendations**
   - Connected customer sentiment with performance metrics
   - Proposed actionable business improvements

### **Key Insights(Overview)**

**Decreased Conversion Rates:**
  - The conversion rate demonstrated a strong rebound in December, reaching 12.8%, despite a notable dip to 4.5% in October.

**Reduced Customer Engagement:**
  - There is a decline in overall social media engagement, with views dropping throughout the year.
  - While clicks and likes are low compared to views, the click-through rate stands at 6.17%, meaning that some engaged users are still interacting effectively.

**Customer Feedback Analysis:**
  - Customer ratings have remained consistent, averaging around 3.66 throughout the year.
  - Although stable, the average rating is below the target of 4.0, suggesting a need for focused improvements in customer satisfaction, for products below 3.5.

##  Decreased Conversion Rates

**General Conversion Trend:**
  - Throughout the year, conversion rates varied, with higher numbers of products converting successfully in months like March and June. This suggests that while some products had strong seasonal peaks, there is potential to improve conversions in lower-performing months through targeted interventions.

**Lowest Conversion Month:**
  - July experienced the lowest overall conversion rate at 4.3%, with no products standing out significantly in terms of conversion. This indicates a potential need to revisit marketing strategies or promotions during this period to boost performance.

**Highest Conversion Rates:**
  - September recorded the highest overall conversion rate at 15.2%, driven significantly by the Hockey stick with a remarkable 100% conversion. This indicates a strong third quarter to the year, likely fueled by seasonal demand and effective marketing strategies.

## Reduced Customer Engagement

**Declining Views:**
   - Views kept declining progressively from Januaray and on, indicating reduced audience engagement even in the later half of the year.

**Low Interaction Rates:**
   - Clicks and likes remained consistently low compared to views, suggesting the need for more engaging content or stronger calls to action.

**Content Type Performance:**
   - Blog content drove the most views, just in January and followed a Somewhat stable level between February and April, while social media and video content kept lowering in "pari passu".

## Customer Feedback Analysis

**Customer Ratings Distribution:**
   - The majority of customer reviews are in the higher ratings, with 150 reviews at 4 stars and 138 reviews at 5 stars, indicating overall positive feedback. Lower ratings (1-2 stars) account for a smaller proportion, with 27 reviews at 1 star and 60 reviews at 2 stars.

**Sentiment Analysis:**
   - Positive sentiment dominates with 292 reviews, reflecting a generally satisfied customer base. Negative sentiment is present in 81 reviews, with a smaller number of mixed and neutral sentiments, suggesting some areas for improvement but overall strong customer approval.

**Opportunity for Improvement:**
   - The presence of mixed positive and mixed negative sentiments suggests that there are opportunities to convert those mixed experiences into more clearly positive ones, potentially boosting overall ratings. Addressing the specific concerns in mixed reviews could elevate customer satisfaction

## Goals & Actions

### GOALS

**Increase Conversion Rates:**
       - Goal: Identify factors impacting the conversion rate and provide recommendations to improve it.
       - Insight: Highlight key stages where visitors drop off and suggest improvements to optimize the conversion funnel.

**Enhance Customer Engagement:**
       - Goal: Determine which types of content drive the highest engagement. 
       - Insight: Analyze interaction levels with different types of marketing content to inform better content strategies.

**Improve Customer Feedback Scores:**
       - Goal: Understand common themes in customer reviews and provide actionable insights.
       - Insight: Identify recurring positive and negative feedback to guide product and service improvements.

### ACTIONS

**Increase Conversion Rates:**
       - Target High-Performing Product Categories: Focus marketing efforts on products with demonstrated high conversion rates, such as Hockey Sticks, Clibing Ropes, Surfboard and Cycling  Helmet. Implement seasonal promotions or personalized campaigns during peak months (e.g., January and September) to capitalize on these trends.

**Enhance Customer Engagement:**
       - Revitalize Content Strategy: To turn around declining views and low interaction rates, experiment with more engaging content formats, such as interactive videos or user-generated content. Additionally, boost engagement by optimizing call-to-action placement in social media and blog content, particularly during historically lower-engagement months (September-December).

**Improve Customer Feedback Scores:**
       - Address Mixed and Negative Feedback: Implement a feedback loop where mixed and negative reviews are analyzed to identify common issues. Develop improvement plans to address these concerns. Consider following up with dissatisfied customers to resolve issues and encourage re-rating, aiming to move average ratings closer to the 4.0 target.
